# The Bible

Everything Régie knows about motion design, in French. Claude reads it before each action; the app shows it as a visual library. More than 300,000 words of method and technique, plus 7,159 rules from 198 open-source video skills.

## What's inside

| Folder | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `prompt-motion/synthese/c01` … `c14` | 14 chapters, 175 entries: styles, composition, typography, transitions, camera and 3D, shapes and materials, data and interfaces, characters, timing and sound, structure, consistency and clichés, production method, writing a prompt, 60 fps frame-by-frame studies. Each entry says what the technique is, when to use it, how to build it in HyperFrames (GSAP) and in After Effects, with the videos where it works best. |
| `prompt-motion/synthese/methode` | The production method Régie follows: the 10-phase route (A), 6 style guides (B), 14 shot recipes (C) and 10 tools (E): studio folder, render contract, timeline tokens, corrections, critique, contact sheets, sound, log, and Blender for heavy 3D shots. |
| `prompt-motion/synthese/regles-or.md` | The golden rules, the short version of everything above. |
| `prompt-motion/analyses` | One analysis per reference video (233): what it does well, what to steal, its flaws, its palette, its rhythm and music sync, its techniques. |
| `prompt-motion/sources.json` | Where each reference video lives on prompt-motion.com. The app plays them from there. |
| `awesome-video-skills/apports` | 7,159 rules taken from 198 open-source Claude video skills and one article, one per line, filed by chapter (`c01.md` … `c14.md`); an index of 2,118 prompts with links; a card per repository (how it is built, its checks, its tools). |
| `regie/savoir-faire-hyperframes.md` | HyperFrames pitfalls already met, read by Claude before it builds. |

## How it was made

1. **233 videos watched frame by frame.** Every video on [prompt-motion.com](https://prompt-motion.com) (all made with Claude) was analysed by Claude agents: contact sheets at 2 frames per second, every native frame around the key events, beat-marked sheets to check whether the animation really follows the music. About 20 flagship videos got a continuous 60 fps study.
2. **14 chapters written from the analyses.** Techniques were grouped across videos, named in French and English, and given a recipe and a "when to use it".
3. **A method on top.** Three articles on Claude motion workflows and a prompt pack gave the process; the Bible's measurements gave the numbers. Each step was tested on real films.
4. **198 open-source skills read.** The relevant repositories listed by [awesome-claude-video-skills](https://github.com/zhuyansen/awesome-claude-video-skills) were read in 10 batches. Rules already in the Bible were set aside; new ones and refinements were rephrased and filed by chapter, each with a link to its source file.
